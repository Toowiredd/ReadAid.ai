# ReadAid.ai Feature Guide

## 🎯 Overview of Enhanced Features

ReadAid.ai has been significantly expanded with new features that enhance user experience, customization, and accessibility. This guide explains what each feature does, how to use it, and why it benefits users with Dyslexia and ADHD.

---

## ✨ New Features (Version 2.0)

### 1. **Reading Level Customization** 📚

**What it does:**
Adjusts the complexity and language level of generated content to match your reading ability.

**How to use:**
- Open the sidebar (⚙️ Settings & Features)
- Under "Reading Preferences", select your reading level:
  - **Beginner**: Very simple language, like teaching a child (ages 8-12)
  - **Intermediate**: Clear language for general audience (default)
  - **Advanced**: Standard language with technical terms

**Why it's useful:**
- Readers with varying skill levels can access the same information
- Beginners won't be overwhelmed by complex jargon
- Advanced readers can get detailed technical explanations
- Helps build confidence in learning new topics

**Example:**
- Topic: "Explain quantum computing"
- **Beginner**: "Quantum computers are special computers that work differently from regular computers..."
- **Advanced**: "Quantum computing leverages quantum mechanical phenomena such as superposition and entanglement..."

---

### 2. **Content Length Options** 📏

**What it does:**
Controls how much detail and explanation is provided in the generated content.

**How to use:**
- In the sidebar, select your preferred content length:
  - **Brief**: Quick summary in 3-5 key points
  - **Moderate**: Balanced explanation with main points (default)
  - **Detailed**: Comprehensive information with examples

**Why it's useful:**
- Short attention span? Choose Brief for quick facts
- Need more context? Choose Detailed for thorough explanations
- Prevents cognitive overload from too much information
- Helps users learn at their own pace

**Example:**
- Topic: "What is photosynthesis?"
- **Brief**: 5 bullet points covering the basics
- **Moderate**: 2-3 paragraphs with key concepts
- **Detailed**: Full explanation with examples and diagrams description

---

### 3. **Font Size Adjustment** 🔤

**What it does:**
Lets you change the size of text to what's comfortable for your eyes.

**How to use:**
- In the sidebar, use the "Font Size" slider
- Range: 12px to 24px (default: 16px)
- Changes apply immediately to the interface

**Why it's useful:**
- Larger fonts reduce eye strain
- Essential for users with visual processing difficulties
- Customizable for individual comfort
- Helps with focus and reading speed

**Best practices:**
- Start with 16px and adjust based on comfort
- Larger fonts (18-20px) often work best for dyslexic readers
- Smaller fonts (14px) may work for quick scanning

---

### 4. **Background Color Selection** 🎨

**What it does:**
Changes the background color of the entire application.

**How to use:**
- In the sidebar, select from four color options:
  - **Yellow** (#fdfd96): High contrast, classic (default)
  - **Light Blue** (#E8F4F8): Calming, reduces glare
  - **Light Gray** (#F0F0F0): Neutral, easy on eyes
  - **Cream** (#FFF9E6): Warm, soft contrast

**Why it's useful:**
- Different colors work better for different people
- Reduces visual stress and eye fatigue
- Yellow backgrounds help many dyslexic readers
- Blue tones can improve focus for ADHD users

**Tips:**
- Try each color for 5 minutes to find your preference
- Yellow is great for high contrast
- Blue/Cream are good for longer reading sessions

---

### 5. **Content History** 📚

**What it does:**
Automatically saves your last 5 searches so you can review them later.

**How to use:**
- Look in the sidebar under "Content History"
- Click on any previous search to see its details
- Each entry shows:
  - The query/topic
  - When it was generated
  - Option to bookmark it

**Why it's useful:**
- Don't lose work if you accidentally close the tab
- Review multiple topics easily
- Compare different searches
- No need to regenerate the same content

**Features:**
- Stores up to 5 recent queries in current session
- Shows timestamp for each search
- Quick bookmark button for each item

---

### 6. **Bookmark System** ⭐

**What it does:**
Saves important or favorite topics for easy access later.

**How to use:**
- After generating content, click "Add to Bookmarks"
- OR click the bookmark button in the history section
- To load a bookmark:
  - Go to sidebar "Bookmarks" section
  - Select from dropdown
  - Click "Load Bookmarked Content"

**Why it's useful:**
- Save topics you want to review regularly
- Build a personal learning library
- No need to remember or retype queries
- Perfect for ongoing learning topics

**Use cases:**
- Study materials for exams
- Topics you're learning about
- References you need frequently
- Content to share with others

---

### 7. **Multiple Export Formats** 💾

**What it does:**
Download generated content in different file formats.

**How to use:**
After content is generated, choose from:
- **Download HTML**: Full formatted content with colors and styles
- **Download TXT**: Plain text version without formatting

**Why it's useful:**
- **HTML**: Keep all the accessibility formatting
- **TXT**: Use in other apps, share via email, or print
- Save content for offline reading
- Share with teachers, tutors, or family

**When to use each:**
- **HTML**: For reading on your computer with full formatting
- **TXT**: For compatibility with other tools or simpler reading

---

### 8. **Reading Statistics** 📊

**What it does:**
Tracks your usage of ReadAid.ai during your session.

**How to use:**
- View in the sidebar under "Reading Statistics"
- Shows:
  - **Total Queries**: How many topics you've searched
  - **Bookmarks Saved**: Number of bookmarked items
  - **Session Duration**: How long you've been using the app

**Why it's useful:**
- See your learning progress
- Track how much you've accomplished
- Motivating to see numbers grow
- Helps manage reading time

**Tips:**
- Use session duration to plan breaks
- Set goals for number of queries
- Celebrate your learning progress!

---

### 9. **Popular Topics & Quick Access** 🚀

**What it does:**
Provides quick access to common topics and your recent searches.

**How to use:**
- Switch to the "Quick Access" tab
- Choose from:
  - **Popular Topics**: Pre-selected common topics
  - **Recent History**: Your last 5 searches
- Click "Load Selected Topic" to generate

**Why it's useful:**
- No typing required for common topics
- Quick review of recent searches
- Reduces cognitive load of thinking what to search
- Great for exploring new topics

**Popular topics included:**
- What is artificial intelligence?
- Explain photosynthesis
- How does the internet work?
- What causes earthquakes?
- Explain the water cycle

---

### 10. **Enhanced Help Section** ℹ️

**What it does:**
Provides comprehensive guidance on using all features.

**How to use:**
- Click the "ℹ️ How to use ReadAid.ai" expander
- Left column: Core features and basic usage

**Why it's useful:**

## 🔄 How Features Work Together

### Example Workflow:

1. **Set Your Preferences** (First time)
   - Choose Beginner reading level
   - Select Moderate content length
   - Adjust font to 18px
   - Pick Light Blue background

2. **Search and Learn**
   - Enter "What is climate change?"
   - Generate content
   - Read the accessible, formatted result

3. **Save for Later**
   - Like the content? Click "Add to Bookmarks"
   - Want to read offline? Download as HTML or TXT

4. **Continue Learning**
   - Check History for your recent searches
   - Try a Popular Topic from Quick Access
   - Adjust reading level as needed

5. **Track Progress**
   - Check Statistics to see your achievement
   - Feel motivated by the numbers!

## 💡 Tips for Best Experience

### For Dyslexic Readers:
- Use **Beginner or Intermediate** reading level
- Choose **Yellow or Cream** background
- Set font size to **18-20px**
- Use **Brief or Moderate** content length
- Take advantage of the **bold first letters** in sentences

### For ADHD Users:
- Start with **Brief** content length to avoid overwhelm
- Use **Light Blue** background for calming effect
- Bookmark interesting topics to return to later
- Check Statistics to feel accomplished
- Use Quick Access for impulsive topic exploration

### For All Users:
- Experiment with settings to find your preference
- Save your favorite settings as your defaults
- Use bookmarks to build a learning library
- Download important content for offline access
- Review history when you need to recall something
